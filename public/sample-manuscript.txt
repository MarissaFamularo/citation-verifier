Revascularization strategy in chronic limb-threatening ischemia: a sample manuscript

Introduction
Chronic limb-threatening ischemia carries a high risk of amputation and death. In patients with an adequate great saphenous vein, surgical bypass first resulted in fewer major adverse limb events or deaths than endovascular therapy first [1]. In contrast, a UK trial found that a best endovascular treatment first strategy was associated with better amputation-free survival than vein bypass first [2].

Discussion
Endovascular repair of abdominal aortic aneurysm has an early survival benefit over open repair, but this advantage is lost in later follow-up [3]. Statin therapy eliminates the risk of amputation after bypass surgery [1].
